About CHHOYED

CHHOYED is a free place to learn coding, math and physics, from beginner to advanced.

What is on the site

The site has 114 lessons in 12 learning paths:

  • Before You Code: 15 lessons (The foundation guide for future web and mobile app developers)
  • HTML: 8 lessons (Learn HTML, build the structure of web pages)
  • CSS: 8 lessons (Learn CSS, make your pages beautiful and responsive)
  • JavaScript: 10 lessons (Learn JavaScript, make your pages interactive)
  • SQL: 8 lessons (Learn SQL, store data and ask it questions)
  • Git & GitHub: 7 lessons (Learn Git and GitHub, save your code and publish your website)
  • Java: 11 lessons (Learn Java, from Hello World to OOP)
  • Java AWT: 5 lessons (Build windows and buttons with Java AWT)
  • Python: 11 lessons (Learn Python, from Hello World to OOP)
  • C++: 11 lessons (Learn C++, from Hello World to OOP)
  • Integral calculus: 12 lessons (Integral Book: 500 exercises with answers)
  • Limits: 3 lessons (Limits: exercises with full solutions)

How to learn here

  • Follow the lessons in order. Each course page shows your progress.
  • Try the code yourself. In the HTML, CSS and JavaScript lessons, press the Try it button on a code block, change the code and see the result at once.
  • Test yourself. Most courses have a Practice page with questions and instant feedback.
  • Start from the beginning if you are new. The course "Before You Code" explains computers, the web, apps and programming, so that every later lesson makes sense.

Your progress is saved only in your own browser. You do not need an account.

Who writes the lessons

Every lesson is posted by the CHHOYED team. The integral and limit books are by our math teacher, ផាត់ ភារិន, with full worked solutions for every exercise.

Contact us

Found a mistake, or want a lesson on a new topic? Use the contact form and we will reply to your email. If a lesson helped you, or confused you, please press the Was this lesson helpful? button at the bottom of the lesson. It tells us what to improve.

In a right triangle, the longest side is called the **hypotenuse**. It is always opposite the right angle.

## The rule

`a² + b² = c²`

`a` and `b` are the two shorter sides. `c` is the hypotenuse.

## Worked example

A ladder leans against a wall. The wall is 4 m high and the ladder's foot is 3 m from the wall. How long is the ladder?

1. `c² = 3² + 4²`
2. `c² = 9 + 16 = 25`
3. `c = 5`

The ladder is **5 m** long.

## Finding a shorter side

If you know `c` and one short side, subtract instead: `a² = c² − b²`.

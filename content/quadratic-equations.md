A quadratic equation looks like this:

`ax² + bx + c = 0`

## The quadratic formula

`x = (−b ± √(b² − 4ac)) / 2a`

The part under the square root, `b² − 4ac`, tells you how many answers there are:

- Positive: two answers
- Zero: one answer
- Negative: no real answers

## Worked example

Solve `x² − 5x + 6 = 0`.

1. Here `a = 1`, `b = −5`, `c = 6`.
2. `b² − 4ac = 25 − 24 = 1`
3. `x = (5 ± 1) / 2`
4. So `x = 3` or `x = 2`.

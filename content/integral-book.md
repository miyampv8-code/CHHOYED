A collection of 500 integral calculus exercises, each with a full worked solution. Compiled and written by ផាត់ ភារិន.

## What is inside

- A foreword and a short history of integral calculus.
- Important relations and formulas used in the solutions.
- **500 exercises** (001 to 500). Each one shows the integral, a step-by-step solution and a boxed final answer.
- Extra research exercises with answers, for students who want more practice.

## How to use it

1. Pick an exercise and try it on your own first.
2. Compare your steps with the solution.
3. Read the yellow **Use** and **Note** boxes. They show the rule or property behind a key step, so you can reuse it in other exercises.

The book is a PDF of 395 pages. Open it in your browser or download it with the buttons above.

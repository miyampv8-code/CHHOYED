Isaac Newton described how forces change motion in three simple laws.

## First law: inertia

An object keeps doing what it is doing unless a force acts on it. A ball on the floor stays still until you kick it. A rolling ball stops because of friction.

## Second law: force and acceleration

`F = m × a`

The bigger the force, the bigger the acceleration. The bigger the mass, the smaller the acceleration. Pushing an empty cart is easier than pushing a full one.

## Third law: action and reaction

When you push on something, it pushes back on you with an equal force in the opposite direction. This is how a swimmer moves forward by pushing water backward.

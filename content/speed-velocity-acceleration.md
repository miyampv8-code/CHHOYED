These three words sound alike, but each means something different.

## Speed

How fast you move, no matter the direction.

`speed = distance / time`

A bus that travels 120 km in 2 hours has a speed of 60 km/h.

## Velocity

Speed **with a direction**. "60 km/h north" is a velocity. Two cars with the same speed but different directions have different velocities.

## Acceleration

How quickly velocity changes.

`acceleration = (final velocity − start velocity) / time`

A bike goes from 0 to 6 m/s in 3 seconds:

`a = (6 − 0) / 3 = 2 m/s²`

## Try it

A car speeds up from 10 m/s to 30 m/s in 5 seconds. Find its acceleration.
